# Recorded impact sounds · CC0-1.0

The WAV files in this directory are edited excerpts of these recordings:

- `racket-hit.wav`: **Ping pong ball hit** by **michorvath**, <https://freesound.org/people/michorvath/sounds/269718/>.
- `table-bounce-1.wav`, `table-bounce-2.wav`, `table-bounce-3.wav`: **Ping Pong - Ball on Table** by **el_boss**, <https://freesound.org/people/el_boss/sounds/628637/>.

Both authors released their recordings under **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal**. These excerpts retain that dedication; they are exceptions to the repository's MIT asset license. Attribution is included to credit the recordists and make the sources traceable.

[CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) · [Legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode)

The source preview URLs, hashes, trim times and processing steps are in `manifest.json`. Processing: mono downmix, isolated transients, room-rumble filtering, short edge fades and peak normalization. No unrelated sounds were added to the recordings.
